Warm-Up Protocol

Perform before every run.

00:00

1. March in Place

1 minute

A-skips in place. Swing arms front-to-back.

2. Leg Swings

30 sec/leg

Hold a wall. Swing one leg forward/backward.

3. Clutch & Lunge

10 reps/leg

Lunge forward while clutching hands to chest.

4. Hip Circles

10 reps/dir

Hands on hips. Make wide, slow circles.

5. Toy Soldiers

10 reps/leg

Straight-leg march. Kick leg up to opposite hand.

6. Open Gate

30 seconds

Lift knee and rotate hip outward.

7. Butt Kicks

30 seconds

Jog in place, kicking heels up toward glutes.

8. Hamstring

5-10 circles

Perform circles in each direction.
