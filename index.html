Training Phasing

Weeks 1-4

Phase 1: The Engine Build

Focus on establishing a consistent aerobic base in the heat. Getting used to time on feet without heart rate spiking.

Weeks 5-8

Phase 2: Threshold & Strength

Teaching your body to clear fatigue while running fast. Introducing "Zone 4" intervals to boost lactate threshold.

Weeks 9-12

Phase 3: Race Sharpening

Locking in the 5K Goal Pace (6:00/km). Volume reduces slightly while intensity increases to simulate race day.
